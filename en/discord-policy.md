## 1. A place to talk together

The official Archer Raising Discord is where you can follow update news, share card combinations and growth tips, and give your opinions about the game.

Everything from a newcomer's first question to a long-time player's criticism is welcome. Please respect different play styles and opinions.

This policy applies to use of the community, including messages, images, files, voice chat, display names, and profiles. Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, and applicable laws, also apply.

## 2. Sharing questions, guides, and opinions

- Post in line with each channel's description and purpose.
- Guides and opinions are more helpful when facts, guesses, and personal experience are kept apart.
- Do not belittle others for their choices or how fast they progress.
- Even when the same question comes up again, do not mock the person or gang up on them.
- Complaints and disagreement may be expressed. Harassing someone is restricted.

The moderators do not delete posts or sanction members merely for negative reviews of the game, legitimate complaints, or improvement requests.

## 3. What not to post in public channels

### Content that harms others

Personal attacks using abusive language, sexual harassment, hate or discrimination, threats, stalking, repeated mockery, and ostracism are prohibited.

Posting or sharing seriously harmful content such as sexual exploitation material, illegally recorded material, malicious files, and phishing links is prohibited.

Repeatedly disrupting a channel's purpose with unrelated topics (for example, repeatedly dragging in political or religious disputes) may be restricted after a warning. The topics themselves are not banned; the repeated disruptive behavior is.

### Personal information and private materials

Do not post or demand someone else's real name, contact details, address, credentials, payment information, or similar without their consent.

Submit chat logs and screenshots needed for a report through a private inquiry. When posting materials in a public channel, mask other people's personal information and sensitive content.

### Flooding, advertising, impersonation

Repeating the same or similar posts, excessive mentions, disrupting voice channels, and stirring up conflict with multiple accounts are prohibited.

Unauthorized commercial advertising, flooding invite links, direct-message advertising aimed at members, and scams are prohibited. Sharing links to legitimate guides and related information is distinguished from advertising.

Impersonating moderators, forging notices or inquiry replies, and deliberately fabricating or spreading false evidence are prohibited. Simple mistakes are distinguished from deliberate deception.

### Account trading and cheating materials

Selling or lending accounts, paid play-for-hire, and soliciting or brokering cash trades of game currency or items are prohibited.

Distributing, selling, or recommending external macros, Repeat Battle Check bypass tools, or game tampering tools is prohibited. Do not publish specific bug-abuse steps that allow abnormal rewards either.

Questions and guides about the automatic features the game itself provides are allowed.

## 4. Reporting bugs and suspected cases

For ordinary bugs, the situation and time, the steps to reproduce, and screenshots help us check.

Please send the following through a private inquiry:

- Bugs that allow currency duplication or claiming rewards more than once
- Security-related problems such as bypassing the Repeat Battle Check
- Materials suggesting a specific member is cheating
- Materials that include payment records or personal data

The operations team handles materials only as far as needed and does not expose reporters or encourage public blame. Materials provided with a report are kept only for the handling of that case and the appeal period, and are deleted afterwards.

A good-faith report that turns out to be mistaken is never by itself grounds for sanctions. Deliberately fabricated evidence or false reports intended to harm someone may be restricted.

Do not repeatedly demand punishment of a specific person in public channels or incite group attacks. Submit additional materials through the inquiry channel.

## 5. Measures to protect the conversation

After reviewing the violation and the harm, moderators may delete messages, warn, restrict chat, remove the member from the server, or impose a permanent ban.

| Situation | Standard measure |
|---|---|
| Wrong channel, minor flooding, and similar | Guidance or a warning; messages deleted if needed |
| Repeating the same violation after guidance | 24-hour chat restriction |
| Repeated harassment or deliberately provoking conflict | 7-day chat restriction |
| Continuing after a restriction, or evading one | Removal from the server or a permanent ban |
| Phishing, malicious files, serious leaks of personal information, sexual exploitation material, serious threats | Immediate permanent ban possible |

Repetition is judged against measures confirmed within the last year.

The level may be adjusted for intent, repetition, harm, and behavior after a measure. To stop urgent harm, content may be deleted or use restricted first, with the reason explained afterwards.

Members unrelated to the problem are not sanctioned together. Whether you have paid, know the moderators, or are active on the server makes no difference to the standard applied.

## 6. Discord sanctions and game use

Discord restrictions and game restrictions are judged under their own policies.

Being removed from the server does not by itself suspend your Archer Raising account.

However, account trading, distribution of cheat tools, bug-abuse materials, or similar confirmed on the server may be investigated separately as grounds for a game policy violation. When a game sanction is applied, the policy and reason are explained.

Rejoining with another account to avoid a server restriction, or continuing the violating behavior through another member, is prohibited.

## 7. Objecting to a moderation action

If you need an explanation of a sanction or a review, please contact the official inquiry channel.

Your Discord user ID, the action taken, the reason for your objection, and related materials help us check. Never send passwords or verification codes.

We recommend filing within 15 days of being notified. Later objections can still be reviewed when new evidence or a clear error is found.

If you cannot access the server, you can use "Contact the Creator" on MapleStory Worlds.

A legitimate appeal never leads to additional sanctions. If an investigation finds that an action was wrong, it is corrected.

## 8. Notices and rule changes

The Game Operations Policy, this Discord Operations Policy, and major changes are posted where anyone can easily find them. The full game policy is also provided at a public address readable without joining Discord.

The notice period for rule changes, keeping previous versions, and the ban on retroactive application follow section 10 of the Game Operations Policy. Urgent removal of harmful posts and measures to prevent harm may be carried out immediately under the rules in force.
