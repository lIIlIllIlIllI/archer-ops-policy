## 1. Play standards we share

Archer Raising is a game where you grow your character and equipment through repeated battles and take on higher stages and content with card combinations.

The operations team runs the game so that the progress and challenge records players have built up stay fair. This policy exists to protect normal play and to respond consistently when problems occur.

This policy applies to all use of Archer Raising, including combat, growth, rewards, rankings, the megaphone, and paid products. The official Discord server has its own [Discord Operations Policy](discord-policy.md).

Applicable laws and the terms and policies of MapleStory Worlds take precedence. This policy does not limit any rights you have under the law.

## 2. Automatic features the game provides

Automatic features the game itself provides, such as auto attack, auto hunting, auto card selection, and auto equip, may be used under the conditions of each feature.

Using automatic features, repeating the same stage, or staying connected for a long time is never by itself treated as misuse.

However, automating inputs and requests in ways the game does not provide, or passing the Repeat Battle Check (section 3) by any means other than a person, is not allowed.

Examples of prohibited automation and tampering:

- Using external programs or a device's repeat-input feature to automatically start or restart battles, make selections, or claim rewards
- Automatically answering or bypassing the Repeat Battle Check
- Modifying the game client or its communication to manipulate currency, stats, battle results, or similar
- Sending repeated requests beyond normal use in a way that affects the server or saving
- Creating, selling, distributing, or brokering tools for any of the above

Accessibility aids are judged by how they are actually used and whether they automate play or bypass the check. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, please ask through the official contact channel.

## 3. The Repeat Battle Check

While using repeat battles, a check may appear to confirm normal play.

If you do not answer, or answer incorrectly, the next battle start is held and a re-check or a retry wait may apply. The wait grows with consecutive failures and resets once you pass.

Failing or timing out on the check does not by itself establish misuse, and rewards already received are not taken back.

If further signs such as check bypassing or abnormal requests are found, the related records are reviewed together. If a display or input problem makes the check hard to complete, please contact us with the time and situation.

## 4. Behavior that harms growth and challenge records

### Obtaining rewards through bugs

If you happen to encounter a bug, please let us know through the official contact channel. Finding a bug, or reporting it in good faith, is never grounds for sanctions.

However, the following may be restricted once you are aware that something is a bug. Repeating the same bug three or more times, or deliberately reproducing it for gain, is treated as being aware.

- Claiming rewards more than once or abnormally increasing currency or items
- Repeating battle, entry, or save errors for gain
- Hiding gains obtained through a bug or converting them into other forms
- Publishing or distributing specific steps or tools that enable abuse

The minimum checking needed to prevent harm is distinguished from deliberate, repeated abuse.

### Manipulating rankings and records

Registering or keeping stage, Mu Lung, raid, or other records made by illegitimate means is prohibited.

If a record looks abnormal, it may be temporarily removed from the ranking while it is checked. Records confirmed as legitimate are restored; records confirmed as illegitimate are deleted from the ranking.

### Trading accounts and game results

Selling, lending, or sharing accounts, paid play-for-hire, and the cash trading or brokering of game currency, items, or other results are prohibited. Where official purchase or gifting features exist, use them under the conditions those features state.

### Abusing payments and operational procedures

Forging payment records, abusing product delivery errors, deliberately submitting false materials, attacking the service, and other acts that interfere with normal payments and operations are prohibited.

Legitimate refund requests, bug reports, criticism of operations, and appeals are not covered by this.

## 5. The megaphone and protecting players

The megaphone is a space many players see together. Posting the following may lead to a megaphone usage restriction:

- Abuse aimed at a specific person, repeated mockery, or harassment
- Sexual harassment, hate or discrimination, threats, or content related to sexual exploitation
- Repeated posting of the same content or excessive flooding
- Impersonating operators, forging official notices, scams, or phishing
- Publishing someone else's personal information or demanding credentials
- Unauthorized commercial advertising or soliciting account or cash trades
- Distributing cheat programs or bug-abuse methods

Complaints and negative opinions about the game may be expressed. The operations team distinguishes criticism from expressions or behavior that harm others.

## 6. Actions when a problem is confirmed

The operations team acts after reviewing game records, reports, and related circumstances. Whether you have paid, know the operators, or are active in the community makes no difference to the standard applied.

### Available actions

- Guidance or a warning
- Restricting megaphone use (for a set period or permanently)
- Removing abnormal ranking records
- Recovering or correcting confirmed illegitimate gains
- Restricting use of Archer Raising for a set period or permanently

The scope of recovering illegitimate gains is decided from verifiable records and what can actually be processed. Recovery stays within the currency and items the account holds, and unrelated legitimate progress is never reset wholesale.

If you log in while restricted, the restriction, the reason, the scheduled end time, and how to ask or appeal are shown, and once that time passes you can play again without any further step. Megaphone restrictions lift automatically the same way.

### Standard sanctions

| Violation | First confirmed | Repeated violation of the same type |
|---|---|---|
| Minor flooding, abuse, unauthorized advertising | Warning and content removal | Megaphone restriction 3 days → 7 days → 30 days |
| Repeated harassment, impersonation, deliberate interference with operations | Megaphone restriction 7 days | Megaphone 30 days → game 7 days → game 30 days → permanent |
| External automation, check bypassing, deliberate bug abuse, ranking manipulation | Game restriction 30 days | Game 90 days → permanent |
| Account trading, paid play-for-hire, cash trading | Game restriction 30 days | Game 90 days → permanent |

Repetition is judged against actions confirmed within the last year. Actions older than a year are only taken into account as context.

Large-scale currency duplication, attacks on the service, creating, selling, or distributing cheat tools, serious leaks of personal information, and sexual exploitation may lead to a permanent restriction on the first confirmation.

The level may be adjusted for intent, repetition, the scale of harm and gain, and voluntary reporting. When an action lower or higher than the standard is applied, the reason is recorded and explained to the person concerned.

When several items are violated together, the most serious act is the basis for judgment. The same act is not counted twice, and periods are not mechanically added up.

### Temporary measures during an investigation

To stop harm from spreading or to establish the facts, a temporary usage restriction or ranking exclusion may be applied. A temporary measure does not mean a violation has been confirmed, and it is lifted once it is no longer needed.

Links to other accounts are judged on verifiable evidence. Being acquaintances or sharing a similar connection environment is not by itself grounds for joint sanctions.

Confirmed actions are communicated with the item applied, the main reason, the period, and how to appeal. Details that involve personal information or could be used to evade detection may be withheld.

Usage restrictions in Archer Raising apply to this world. Measures against a whole platform account follow the authority and policies of MapleStory Worlds.

## 7. Reward and save problems, and recovery

If you did not receive a reward or your progress disappeared, please contact us as soon as possible.

The operations team checks the time, your profile code, the content you used, and your records, and lets you know whether recovery or re-delivery is possible.

The following requests may be hard to support or limited in scope:

- Losses for which verifiable records are insufficient
- Reversing items that were used, sold, or dismantled normally
- Changing normal card choices or probability outcomes
- Rolling back progress because of a balance change
- Matters outside the operations team's authority, such as platform account recovery

We do not guarantee that all data can be rolled back to a specific point in time. For harm confirmed as caused by a game error or an operational problem, we look at what can actually be done, and if support is not possible we explain why.

We never ask for passwords or verification codes during an inquiry. Materials provided for an inquiry are kept only for the handling of that inquiry and the appeal period, and are deleted afterwards.

## 8. Paid products and refunds

The price, contents, conditions of use, and any probability information subject to disclosure for paid products are shown on the purchase screen or in related notices.

If a product was not delivered or differs from what was described, please use the MapleStory Worlds inquiry and refund features or the official contact channel.

Refunds are handled according to applicable laws, platform policy, the purchase route, and the state of use. Products or benefits provided through the purchase may be withdrawn or disabled following a refund, and we explain what is needed.

Requesting a refund or raising a payment issue is never by itself grounds for sanctions. Forgery or deliberate abuse of the procedure is judged on separate evidence.

Joining Discord is not required to make an inquiry or request a refund.

## 9. Maintenance and game changes

Important matters such as bug fixes, balance changes, content additions or changes, and maintenance are announced through in-game notices, the world description, or the official community.

In an urgent security issue or a serious error, a feature may be restricted first, with the reason and follow-up announced afterwards.

Changes that materially affect the conditions of paid products, or the end of service, are announced separately with a schedule and handling method in accordance with applicable laws and platform policy.

## 10. Inquiries and policy changes

If you object to a sanction, we recommend contacting us within 15 days of being notified. Later objections can still be reviewed when new evidence or a clear error is found.

If you cannot use Discord, you can still use "Contact the Creator" on MapleStory Worlds.

The full policy is published at a public address readable without joining Discord, and the way to find it is shown in the game or in the world description.

When the policy changes, the changes, the reason, and the effective date are announced, and previous versions are kept. As a rule, ordinary changes are announced 7 days before taking effect, and changes that are unfavorable to players or otherwise important are announced 30 days before. Where applicable laws or platform policy set a different procedure, that procedure is followed.

Stricter sanction standards are not applied retroactively to conduct before their effective date.
